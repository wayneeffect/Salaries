**Entry-level Solutions Architect roles (typically requiring prior technical or architecture experience) generally start in the $95,000–$130,000 range nationally in the US; mid-level around $125,000–$170,000; senior levels commonly $150,000–$220,000+ base, with total compensation often reaching $180,000–$300,000+ (and higher at large tech firms) when including bonuses, commissions, and equity.**

These are approximate 2026 US national figures for Solutions Architect (sometimes titled Solution Architect). The role involves designing end-to-end technical solutions that align business needs with technology, often spanning cloud, software, systems, integration, and enterprise architecture. Pure zero-experience entry roles are uncommon—most expect several years of engineering, systems, or related architecture background. Compensation varies significantly by specialization (e.g., cloud, enterprise, industry-specific), company type, location, and total package.

### Key Data Highlights
- **PayScale**: Solutions Architect average base ~$136k (range ~$88k–$180k); Senior Solutions Architect average base ~$151k (range ~$107k–$188k). Entry-level total compensation often starts around $84k–$108k.
- **Glassdoor**: Solutions Architect median total pay often ~$213k–$220k (base commonly $132k–$199k); Senior Solutions Architect median total around $227k.
- **KORE1 / market guides**: Associate/Junior base $95k–$125k; Mid $125k–$160k; Senior $160k–$210k; Principal/Staff $200k–$275k (with total comp higher where equity applies).
- **Levels.fyi and related**: Senior Solution Architect median total ~$230k; overall Solutions Architect medians frequently $200k–$220k total at tech companies.
- **Other notes**: Indeed averages around $155k base from postings. Cloud-focused or multi-cloud roles, especially at AWS/Microsoft/Google, tend to push higher, particularly in total compensation.

### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Tech hubs (Bay Area, Seattle, NYC, Boston) often add 15–30%+ premiums; remote roles may track closer to national averages.
- **Specialization** — Cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), enterprise, security, data, or industry-specific (finance, healthcare) expertise increases value. Multi-cloud or AI-related skills command premiums.
- **Company type** — Large tech, cloud providers, consulting, or finance firms typically offer stronger total compensation (especially equity/RSUs or commissions) than smaller organizations.
- **Scope** — Pre-sales/customer-facing vs. internal; single solutions vs. enterprise-wide architecture.
