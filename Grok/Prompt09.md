**Entry-level Cloud Solutions Architect roles (typically requiring prior cloud, systems, or architecture experience) generally start in the $100,000–$140,000 range nationally in the US; mid-level around $135,000–$180,000; senior levels commonly $160,000–$230,000+ base, with total compensation often reaching $180,000–$300,000+ (and significantly higher at major cloud providers or tech firms) when including bonuses and equity.**

These are approximate 2026 US national figures for Cloud Solutions Architect (also called Cloud Architect or similar). The role involves designing, implementing, and optimizing cloud-based solutions, often across AWS, Azure, or GCP, including architecture, migration, security, and cost optimization. Pure zero-experience entry roles are rare—most expect several years of cloud engineering, systems architecture, or related experience. Pay varies by cloud platform expertise, certifications, company (hyperscalers pay more in total comp), location, and package structure.

### Key Data Highlights
- **PayScale**: Cloud Solutions Architect average base ~$132k (range ~$86k–$181k); entry-level total compensation around $90k.
- **Glassdoor**: Cloud Solutions Architect median total pay often ~$230k–$232k (base commonly $131k–$182k); Senior Cloud Solutions Architect median total around $207k.
- **Levels.fyi / related**: Cloud Architect median total compensation ~$220k; higher at companies like Amazon (Solution Architect levels can range $150k–$400k+ total depending on level).
- **KORE1 and market guides**: Associate/Cloud Architect base often $130k–$200k; Senior $190k–$235k; Principal/Lead higher, with total comp substantially elevated where equity is offered.
- **Other notes**: AWS-focused roles frequently cite midpoints near $150k–$155k total; multi-cloud or specialized skills (AI integration, security) command premiums.

### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Bay Area, Seattle, NYC, and other tech hubs often add 15–30%+ premiums; remote roles may be closer to national medians or location-adjusted.
- **Platform & skills** — Deep expertise in AWS, Azure, or GCP (especially Professional-level certifications), multi-cloud, security, cost optimization, Well-Architected frameworks, or AI/ML integration increases value.
- **Company type** — Cloud providers (AWS, Microsoft, Google), large tech, finance, or consulting firms typically offer stronger total compensation than smaller organizations.
- **Scope** — Single-workload design vs. enterprise-scale architecture, customer-facing (pre-sales) vs. internal, or hybrid cloud/on-prem solutions.
