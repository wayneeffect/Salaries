**Entry-level System Architect roles (typically requiring prior systems or related engineering experience) generally start in the $100,000–$140,000 range nationally in the US; mid-level around $130,000–$170,000; senior levels commonly $150,000–$220,000+ base, with total compensation often reaching $180,000–$280,000+ (and higher at large tech or specialized firms) when including bonuses and equity.**

These are approximate 2026 US national figures synthesized from PayScale, Glassdoor, Indeed, Salary.com, SalaryExpert, and related sources. System (or Systems) Architect roles focus on high-level design of IT systems, platforms, infrastructure, integration, and overall technology architecture. Pure zero-experience entry roles are uncommon—most expect several years of systems engineering, IT architecture, or related experience. Compensation varies by specialization (e.g., enterprise systems, cloud platforms, infrastructure), industry, location, and total package.

- **Other notes**: SalaryExpert averages ~$111k–$124k overall (entry ~$79k–$88k, senior ~$128k–$143k in some reports). Specialized or enterprise roles, especially in tech/finance, push higher.

### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Tech hubs (Bay Area, Seattle, NYC, DC area) often add 20–40%+ premiums; remote roles frequently track nearer national medians.
- **Skills & domain** — Enterprise architecture, cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP), systems integration, infrastructure, security, scalability, or hybrid hardware-software systems increase value.
- **Company type** — Large tech, finance, defense/aerospace, or consulting firms typically offer stronger total compensation than smaller organizations.
- **Scope** — Application/system-level vs. enterprise-wide architecture; hands-on design vs. strategic oversight.
