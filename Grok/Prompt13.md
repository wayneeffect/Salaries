**AI-Assisted Product Engineer** (also appearing as AI Product Engineer or similar) is an emerging role that blends product engineering with heavy use of AI tools—building AI-powered features, integrating LLMs, rapid prototyping with AI coding assistants, evaluation frameworks, and end-to-end product ownership. Compensation sits at a premium relative to traditional product or full-stack engineering due to high demand.

**Approximate good starting salaries (US national, 2026 data):**

- **Entry-level / Junior** (0–2/3 years): $110,000–$150,000 base/total (often $110k–$160k total). Pure entry roles are less common; most expect some engineering or product experience.
- **Mid-level** (2–5/6 years): $150,000–$200,000 base/total (commonly $150k–$195k).
- **Senior-level** (5+ years): $180,000–$260,000+ base/total (frequently $180k–$260k, with higher packages at strong tech companies reaching $250k–$350k+ total compensation including equity).

### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Bay Area, NYC, Seattle often higher; remote roles may adjust or match national medians.
- **Skills** — LLM integration, RAG, prompt engineering, AI evaluation, full-stack delivery with AI tools (e.g., Cursor, Claude), product judgment, and production AI systems.
- **Company type** — AI-native startups, big tech, or well-funded product companies offer stronger total packages (especially equity).
- **Scope** — Feature implementation vs. owning AI product direction and reliability.
