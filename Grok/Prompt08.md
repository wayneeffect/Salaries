### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Tech hubs (Seattle, Bay Area, NYC, Northern Virginia for AWS) often pay more; remote roles vary but can track national medians.
- **Certifications & skills** — AWS, Azure, or GCP certifications, scripting/automation, networking, security, and multi-cloud experience boost offers.
- **Company** — Hyperscalers (Amazon AWS, Microsoft, Google) and large tech firms typically offer higher total compensation (base + stock + bonus) than smaller MSPs or enterprises.
- **Scope** — Basic ticket/support vs. advanced troubleshooting, architecture input, or specialized cloud services.
