**Entry-level Network Engineer (roughly 0–2/3 years): typically $60,000–$85,000 base nationally in the US; mid-level (about 3–7 years): $90,000–$125,000+; senior (6–10+ years): $120,000–$170,000+, with higher total compensation possible in strong markets, specialized roles, or big tech.**

These are approximate 2026 US national ranges drawn from sources including Glassdoor, PayScale, ZipRecruiter, KORE1 (market offer data), Salary.com, and related aggregators. Note that BLS “Computer Network Architect” figures (median often ~$130k) lean more toward senior/design-focused roles and tend to run higher than pure “Network Engineer” averages. Actual pay varies widely by location, company size/industry, certifications (CCNA/CCNP/CCIE, cloud, security), automation/cloud skills, remote vs. on-site, and total package (base + bonus/equity).

### Key Data Points
- **Glassdoor**: Network Engineer median total pay often ~$123k–$124k (base commonly in the $84k–$130k range); Senior Network Engineer median total around $167k–$171k (base $110k–$173k range in various reports).
- **PayScale**: Network Engineer average base ~$88k (range roughly $60k–$121k); Sr. Network Engineer average base ~$118k (range $85k–$155k). Entry-level totals often start in the mid-$60ks.
- **KORE1 / market guides**: Entry $62k–$81k; mid $95k–$125k; senior $125k–$168k; principal/architect $157k–$218k+. Realistic production-experienced mid-to-senior offers frequently $95k–$145k+.
- **ZipRecruiter / others**: Mid-level averages near $109k; senior figures commonly higher. Some aggregates show overall medians in the $97k–$112k range.
- **Additional context**: Cloud networking, SD-WAN, security, automation (Python/Ansible), and multi-vendor experience add premiums. Big-tech or high-cost areas can push total compensation significantly higher (sometimes $150k–$200k+ including equity).

### Factors That Drive Higher Pay
- **Location** — Tech hubs (Bay Area, Seattle, NYC, DC area) often command 20–40%+ premiums; lower-cost regions pay less. Remote roles typically align closer to national medians.
- **Skills & certifications** — Advanced Cisco (CCNP/CCIE), cloud (AWS/Azure networking), security, automation, and zero-trust/SDN experience boost offers.
- **Employer type** — Large enterprises, tech firms, finance, or government/contracting roles generally pay more than smaller organizations.
- **Scope** — Pure operations vs. design/architecture/hybrid systems-network work.
