**Entry-level Network Admin (roughly 0–2/3 years): typically $55,000–$80,000 base nationally in the US; mid-level (about 3–7/9 years): $80,000–$110,000+; senior (8–10+ years): $100,000–$140,000+, with higher totals possible in strong markets or specialized roles.**

These are approximate 2026 US national ranges compiled from BLS data (for Network and Computer Systems Administrators), Glassdoor, PayScale, Indeed, Robert Half, Salary.com, and other aggregators. Actual figures vary significantly by location, company size/industry, certifications (e.g., CCNA/CCNP), cloud/security skills, remote vs. on-site work, and total compensation (base + bonuses/benefits).

### Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — High-cost metros (Bay Area, NYC, Seattle, DC area) often add 20–40%+; lower-cost or rural areas pay less. Remote roles frequently track closer to national medians.
- **Skills & certifications** — Cisco (CCNA/CCNP/CCIE), cloud networking (AWS/Azure), security, SD-WAN, automation, and multi-vendor experience command premiums.
- **Employer & industry** — Tech, finance, government/contracting, or large enterprises typically pay more than small businesses or education/non-profits.
- **Scope** — Day-to-day operations/admin vs. design, architecture, or hybrid systems/network roles.
